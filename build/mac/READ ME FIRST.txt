COCO WHISPER FOR MAC
Hold a key, talk, let go. Your words appear wherever the cursor is.
Setup takes about two minutes and you only do it once.


IF YOU HAD THE OLD "COCONUT WHISPER" BEFORE
  1. Click the microphone icon at the top right of your screen and choose Quit.
  2. In Applications, drag Coconut Whisper to the Trash.
  That's all. Your settings and the speech model carry over.


INSTALL
  1. In this window, drag Coco Whisper onto the Applications folder.

  2. Open your Applications folder and double click Coco Whisper.
     macOS will say it "could not verify" the app. Click Done (or Cancel).
     (The app is built by our team, not sold on the App Store, so Apple
     asks this once.)

  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom,
     and click "Open Anyway" next to Coco Whisper. Type your Mac password
     if it asks, then click Open.

  4. A small Coco Whisper window appears asking for one permission.
     Click "Ask macOS", then "Open System Settings" in the box macOS shows,
     and switch Coco Whisper on.
     The app closes and reopens by itself. That's expected.

  5. The first time you dictate, macOS asks to use the microphone.
     Click Allow.


USE IT
  Click into any text box (Slack, Gmail, anywhere).
  Hold the RIGHT Command key and talk. Let go when you're done.
  The text appears a second or two later.
  The app lives in the menu bar at the top right, near the clock.
  It has no window and no Dock icon.


UPDATING LATER
  Download the new version, open it, and drag Coco Whisper onto Applications.
  Choose Replace. The permission from step 4 stays in place.
  macOS may ask for "Open Anyway" one more time (step 3).


IF THE KEY DOES NOTHING
  Click the microphone icon in the menu bar and choose Permissions.
  Still stuck? Choose "Open log folder" from the same menu and send
  app.log to Daniel.
